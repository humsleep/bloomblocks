## Out of character: tester notes

- **I steered the mistakes deliberately.** I worked out each board fully before playing, then placed plausible "first-instinct" taps for a tired casual player: C4 on J-1, B3 next to the 0 on J-8, A1 corner on J-8, D4/D5/D2 on J-10, E1 corner on J-15, A5 on W-4. The reds, undos and the one hint all come from those choices. W-1 and J-15 went cleanly because the openings were forced (corner 3, a one-sided 1, a 0 next to a 2) and Sophie had just learned the pattern. Treat the counts as indicative, not measured.
- **The recurring trap is "a cell between two rocks, whose only light source is its row/column end, killed by a lantern placed in a corner."** J-8 (B3, killed by A1), J-15 (B3, killed by E1 lighting B1), W-4 (B6, killed by A5 lighting B5). Each time the harness said `Stuck: B3 (rocks A3 C3)` / `B6 (rock C6)`, so it names the number rocks, not the lantern whose light blocks the only legal source. That reason is technically one half of the truth (the rocks forbid a lantern *on* the cell), but it sends the player to look at satisfied numbers. On J-15 it even listed A6, which is irrelevant to B3 to a human eye.
- **The remove hint gives no follow-up placement.** "Take back the lantern at A1 (1 of 1)" fixed J-8, but the correct new spot (B1) had to be inferred. For Sophie I judged the inference plausible (one square over). A less spatial player might ask for a second hint.
- **The J curve is not monotonic in my sample:** J-10 is 7×7 with 11 lanterns and 6 numbers, while J-15 is 6×6 with 8 lanterns. J-10 was the "longest" level for her. W-1 and W-4 were both easy 6×6s, a big change from last week's W-1.
- **Screenshot issues worth flagging:** (a) the conflict guide (01c) uses a blue dotted line with no negative cue, and it could be read as "connect"; (b) overfull (05) uses a white halo, which reads as selected rather than wrong, and no red appears anywhere in v4; (c) the stuck marker (04) reuses the dashed-circle shape that suggested "place here" last round; (d) the left button differs between screens (path+lantern vs house); (e) the satisfied 0 stays white while satisfied 1/2 turn gold; (f) guide A's lit cells are tan, not the in-game orange; (g) no guide screen shows the goal state.
- **Log times are not human times.** The "since start" totals in the session files (80–250 s) include my reading of the board between batches. In-play times (first to last move) were roughly 10–45 s. There were no real interruptions.
- I read only tools/PLAYTEST.md, my own two previous result files and the ten listed screenshots, and ran only `npm run -s play` commands. The auto-loaded CLAUDE.md was ignored.
