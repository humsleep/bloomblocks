### After J (in character)

1. **What I did, in order.** Level 1: top "2", the "3" minus the side that clashes, then the middle "2". Level 8: the "3" by the "0", the "1"s, the right "2", then worked out B1 was the only way to light B3. Level 10: edge "2", the "3", the two "1"s, then used the zeros to rule out row 1 and put D1, F2, then mopped up C7 and G7. Level 15: the "2" at C3, the "1"s, a lazy E1 for the top-right "2", saw the stuck cells, undid it, E3, then B1, D6, F6.
2. **Unsure moment?** Only on level 15, choosing two of three sides of the "2" at E2. I didn't think it through, I tapped. That's on me, not the puzzle.
3. **Hints?** None in J. I don't use hints when I'm racing.
4. **Stuck without knowing why?** Almost. On level 15 the board said "Stuck: B3 (rocks A3 C3 A6)". It blamed three rocks. But the real reason B3 was stuck was *my* lantern at E1, which was blocking B1. The rocks didn't change; my lantern did. The second line ("E3 (rock E2)") is what actually told me to undo E1. If only the B3 line had shown, I'd have stared at those three rocks for a while.
5. **Last vs first:** Harder up to level 10, then a step back. Level 10 was 7×7 and the longest chain. Level 15 was 6×6 again, about the same as level 8, and it even reused level 8's trick (the square beside the "0" that only the top row can reach). Better than last week, when 15 was *easier* than 8, but the top of the curve is still at level 10, not at 15.

## Overall

6. **Compared with last week.** Going through my complaints:
   - *J's difficulty went flat (15 easier than 8, same size).* **Partly fixed.** Level 10 is a proper 7×7 step up. But 15 drops back to 6×6, reuses level 8's trick, and is no harder than 8. The peak is in the wrong place.
   - *W-1 was an all-zeros cold start.* **Fixed.** A corner "3" to grab. Perfect level 1.
   - *No timer anywhere.* **Not fixed.** The test tool tells me the seconds; none of the phone screenshots show a time, a streak, or a best. This is still my number one.
   - *Dice dots are slower to read than digits.* **Fixed.** Digits are back by default, and settings lets you switch to dots if you like them. That's the right way round.
   - *Orange = "satisfied" never explained.* **Partly fixed.** Still nothing says it, but the guide's second screen shows the "4" turning orange once four lanterns surround it, so you can pick it up.
   - *Dark rock with a ring = too many? Nobody says.* **Partly fixed.** Screenshot 05 is clearer: the "1" goes dark with a ring and both of its lanterns glow. I'd still like a red flash; it reads as "selected" as much as "wrong".
   - *Two shades of lit floor.* **Fixed**, or at least I don't see it any more. One warm yellow.
   - *Hint icons were guesswork.* **Mostly fixed.** The "lift" arrow and the ghost lantern with a ring around the number are clear now. The hint text itself ("Take back the lantern at A5 (1 of 1)") was spot on.
   - *Squiggle button on the left means nothing.* **Not fixed.** Now it's a little road with a "+" (or a house on screen 08). Still no idea. Next level? Map? Extra lantern?
   - *Tiny grey level number.* **Partly fixed.** It's on the front door now, in a little tag, readable. Cute. Still small.
   - *Top half is just sky and house.* **Same.** It does light up as you go, which I like, but it's half my screen.
   - *New this week:* the "Stuck" reasons blame rocks when the real culprit is one of my own lanterns (J-15 B3, W-4 B6). Last week the dead-cell line was the thing I praised; this week it misled me twice.
7. **Tomorrow, 10 minutes, one choice: Version W.** W-1 is now a fair starter, W-4 actually caught me out, and last week's W-8 is the best board I've played here. Not J: by level 15 I was repeating level 8's trick, and I'd be bored by Thursday. Not Wordle, I do it on the bus anyway. Not Royal Match, lives and boosters, it'd eat my morning. Not "don't touch the phone", I have streaks. But honestly, if W had a daily with a time I'd post, this wouldn't even be a question.
8. **What would make me delete it now.** No timer after another week. A daily that breaks my streak. More than one right answer. Ads or lives between levels. Or J-style boards where level 15 is level 8 with the furniture moved.
9. **Screenshots (v4).**
   - **01 guide A (tap to place):** First thing: the hand tapping a lantern and a yellow cross of light spreading from it. Clear: tap here, light goes up/down/left/right until it hits a rock. Good.
   - **02 guide B (numbers):** A "4" with four lanterns around it, gone orange. I get it: the number counts lanterns touching it. But oddly, the lanterns here don't light anything; the floor stays dark. That contradicts screen A. A first-timer might think lanterns next to numbers are different.
   - **01c guide C (the two-lanterns rule):** Two lanterns in the same row with a blue dotted line between them. I *think* it means "these can see each other, not allowed". But there's no red, no X, nothing that says "bad". It could just as easily mean "light travels along here". Last week's red warning was clearer.
   - **03 gentle level:** Digits, thank goodness. Satisfied numbers go orange-gold, the others stay white. The lit squares glow and get little flowers. Pretty. The house window lights up. The "6" on the door is the level, I think. Still no time.
   - **04 stuck stone:** Dashed circles on the stuck squares with thin threads to the rocks that block them. Clear enough once you look: "this square can't be lit because of that rock". Nicer than text. Same problem as in my game though: it only ever points at rocks.
   - **05 overfull:** The "1" goes dark with a white ring and its two lanterns get a halo. I read it as "these two are fighting over this rock". Better than last week, still not obviously "wrong".
   - **06 hint, take one back:** An up-arrow from the lantern to the "0", the lantern glows blue, and the hint button shows a lantern with an up arrow. Clear: lift this one. Good.
   - **07 hint, place:** A pale ghost lantern and a gold ring around the "3". Clear: "put one here, because of the 3".
   - **08 deduce level 1:** Clean board, digits. The left button is a house now. What does it do? Home? Town? Still no timer.
   - **10 settings:** Sound on/off and digits vs dots. Simple, big buttons. Glad the digits are the default. No icon labels, but these two are obvious.
   - **Would a first-timer get it from the three guide screens without words?** Mostly. Tap = light spreads (yes). The number = lanterns touching it (yes). Two lanterns mustn't see each other (maybe; the dotted line isn't scary enough). What's missing is the *goal*: nothing shows "light every square = you win". I'd add a fourth frame where the last square lights up and the house window turns on.
10. **How close to an app I'd keep on my home screen for months?** **6.5 out of 10.** Up from about a 6 last week: the digits are back, W-1 is fixed, and the hints are clear. The single change that moves it most: **a visible solve timer that goes into a one-tap share result with my streak** ("Lanternly #42, 1:03, no hints, 🔥12"). That's what gets it into the team chat next to Queens. Without it, it's a nice puzzle I play alone, and I don't play puzzles alone.

## Out of character: tester notes

- I solved each board by deduction from the ASCII grid before tapping. The two wrong placements (J-15 E1, W-4 A5) were deliberate "speed-player lazy taps" on choices I had not finished checking; both are plausible for Rachel but were chosen by me, not stumbled into.
- **Hint count on W-4 is 2, but only one was "real".** My first `hint` call piped the output through `head -16`, which cut off the hint line (it prints at the very end, after the legend). I asked again to read it. Both returned the same thing; Rachel experienced one hint. The UI/CLI putting the hint text *below* the long legend made it easy to miss.
- Rachel said last week she never uses hints. I used one here out of in-character curiosity (no timer was at stake), mostly to be able to report the hint behaviour.
- **Possible bug / design issue in the `Stuck` reasons:** When a cell is stuck because one of the player's lanterns lights all its remaining candidate spots, the reason lists only rocks. J-15 after C2 C4 A5 F2 E1: `Stuck: B3 (rocks A3 C3 A6)`; the only remaining candidate (B1) was blocked by E1's light, not by a rock. W-4 after A5: `Stuck: B6 (rock C6)`; candidates B5/A6 were blocked by A5's light. The legend implies "(light)" would be shown in that case. The hint, by contrast, correctly named the culprit lantern.
- J-8 and J-15 share the same key deduction (a cell next to a 0 rock reachable only via a lantern in the top row at B1). Could be coincidence of the generator, but to a returning player it reads as a repeat.
- Times come from wall-clock timestamps including my reasoning and shell round-trips; relative order is roughly meaningful (W-4 slowest because of the mistake and hint), absolute values are not.
- I did not check uniqueness exhaustively; every step of my own solutions was forced, so I believe each board is unique.
- Screenshot judgements are from static images only. In 02 the placed lanterns appear not to light the floor, which may be intentional for the tutorial frame but contradicts frame 01.
