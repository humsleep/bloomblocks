## Out of character: tester notes

- **ASCII vs phone:** I had a text board with cell names, a `Stuck:` line naming the dark cells and their cause, and ✓/↑/✗/⊘ per number. The phone shows only the dashed-circle stuck marker and color changes. Real Denise would likely take longer on J-8, J-15 and W-4 and might quit one of them rather than take a hint.
- **Mistakes were partly chosen.** I could solve each board in my head before playing (all six have short forced chains). I played a persona line: start at the biggest number, pick the "obvious" side, ignore zeros, start in corners, light long rows. The errors (C4 on J-1; B3, B4, E3, A1 on J-8; C4, D4, D5, F1 on J-10; E1 on J-15; A2, C5 on W-1; C5, D2, A3, B1, B3 on W-4) were persona-plausible picks, not real confusion.
- **Undo vs tap-off:** I sometimes removed a wrong lantern by tapping it (counted as a move) and sometimes used `undo`. Mixed use was deliberate to reflect a phone without an obvious undo button.
- **Hints:** All three hints were "Take back X (1 of 1)." Each time the persona-natural follow-up (move one square over / fill the dark cell under the 2 / use the corner) completed the board, so hint quality felt better than last week. No "place" hint appeared in my sessions.
- **Difficulty curve:** J-10 (7×7, 39 floor cells, 11 lanterns) is clearly harder and longer than J-15 (6×6, 8 lanterns). The J curve is not monotonic. W-1 this week (6×6, 3 numbers, no zeros) is much gentler than last week's W-1 (four zeros).
- **Recurring pattern:** "Numbers all satisfied, a dark cell no allowed lantern can light" occurred in 3 of 6 levels. The cause each time was a lantern placed outside any number's influence (A1, E1, A3), which the `Stuck:` reason attributes to rocks, not to that lantern. Phone shot 04 has the same issue (strings to rocks, not to the blocking lantern).
- **Screenshot readings are guesses:** I can't tell whether the 05 gray ring means "too many" or the 06 blue glow + up arrow means "remove." I report how she'd read them. The 01c onboarding frame shows no conflict state (no red), so it may not teach the line-of-sight rule.
- **Timing:** Session times (17–46 s) are command latency, not human time.
