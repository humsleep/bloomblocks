2. **A moment I wasn't sure what to do?** Level 8, when all the numbers had gone happy but two squares in the top row were still dark. Nothing on the board said "this lantern is the wrong one". The board says "all good" and "not finished" at the same time.
3. **Hint:** Once, on level 8. It said "remove the lantern at D5", the one I'd put by the "1". Right after, I put it on the other side of the "1" (under it), and that freed up the middle column. Then I saw a lantern at the top would light the whole top row. Three more taps and I was done. It felt like the hint told me *which* mistake, but I still got to do the fixing. I liked that.
4. **Stuck and couldn't tell why?** Only on level 8, as above. On level 3 and 15 I had a dark square too, but I could see *why* (it was next to a zero or a full number), so I knew to move the lantern beside it.
5. **Last level vs first:** A bit harder than level 1, but honestly about the same as level 8, maybe easier. It was still a 6×6, and it opened with a gift (the "3" on the edge). It felt like the game was pacing me nicely rather than ramping up. Last week's level 15 was 7×7 and needed a hint. This one I did myself.

Several levels in a row like W-1 or W-8, where I need a hint just to get moving. Or if hints started costing money or ran out. Or if the town/house thing below turned out not to exist and it was just board after board on a dark screen. The game being hard isn't the problem, as long as it's hard *later*. Being made to feel thick on level 1 is.

## Out of character: tester notes

- **I could read things she couldn't:** I played from the ASCII board. It shows numbers as digits, exact coordinates, and two things the phone may not show: the "Dead cells:" line and the ✓/↑/✗ marks per number. Several times the "Dead cells" line told me a square was already unlightable before I would have noticed. I tried not to act on it until the square was visibly the last dark one. A real Priya on the phone would spot those dead ends later, so she'd likely make more moves and be slower.
- **Solving ability:** I worked out the full solutions of every level before or early in play, which a casual player wouldn't. The mistakes I made are deliberate, persona-plausible ones:
  - wrong three around a "3";
  - picking the wrong side of a "1";
  - the corner habit;
  - placing a lantern beside a "0" to light a dark corner.

  Hints were taken where I judged her patience would run out (J-8, W-1, W-8), not where I was actually stuck. The self-fixes on J-15, W-3 and W-8's final step are realistic but optimistic. A real player might have taken a second hint on W-8, or given up on W-1 or W-8 on a tired evening.
- **Timings:** The logged seconds are command latency. Real thinking time would be several times longer, especially on W-1 and W-8.
- **Structural observations, J vs W:** J started at 5×5 (levels 1 and 3) and was still 6×6 at level 15. W was 6×6 from level 1 and 7×7 at level 8. W-1 had five zeros and a single "1", so there was no forced first move for a beginner. Its unique placements (A2, B1, F1) only follow from reasoning about which cells can still be lit, which is "hard logic" for this persona. J-15 was easier than J-8 for me.
- **Hint design note:** On W-8 the hint removed two lanterns, but the correct next move was the cell I had just tried and undone, because it overfilled two numbers while the wrong lanterns were there. A human might not connect those. In the screenshots, the "remove" hint state seems less self-explanatory than the "place" one.
- **Screenshots:** I could not tell from the images whether conflicts still show in red. Reading the meaning of the orange/white pip colours, the zero ring and the left swirl button is my in-character guess.
