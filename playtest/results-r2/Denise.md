## Out of character: tester notes

- **ASCII vs phone:** I played from a text board with cell names and an explicit status line ("Conflicts: B2 F2", "Dead cells: A1 C1", per-number ✓/↑/✗). A phone player gets none of that text. In particular I saw "Dead cells" which tells you a square is unreachable. Real Denise would not have that, so the "all green, one dark square" trap (J-8, W-3, W-8) would likely confuse her more and for longer than it did me.
- **Mistakes were partly chosen.** I could work out full solutions in advance. I deliberately played a naive persona line (start at the biggest number, pick the "obvious" side, light dark rows, patch). Some errors (C1 on J-1, B3 on J-3, A3 on J-8, B2/B3/D3 on J-15, C3 on W-1, D6 then E6 on W-3, D2/G5 on W-8) were persona-plausible picks, not real confusion. Real Denise would probably make more and different errors.
- **Give-ups:** I completed all 7 levels. A real low-patience player might quit W-3 after the second consecutive "remove" hint, or W-8 (7×7, 8 numbers) before finishing. W-1 being 6×6 with four zeros is a plausible first-session churn point.
- **Timing:** Session times (about 30-60 s per level) are command latency, not human time. Real play on 6×6/7×7 would be several minutes.
- **Hints:** I read every hint in full and followed it exactly. "Remove" hints don't say where the lantern should go next. I filled that gap with reasoning that a casual player may not do. The one "place" hint (J-15) was clearly more helpful.
- **Rules leakage:** I read PLAYTEST.md before playing (light passes over lanterns, numbers count side-adjacent only). I tried to act only on the one-line rule, but I understood conflicts and zeros better than a no-rules player would.
- **Screenshots** are from the gentle track and don't show the J/W boards I played. My reading of the dot/ring stones, orange-when-satisfied dots, and the squiggle button is a guess from the images.
