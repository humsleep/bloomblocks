## Answers (in character)

**1. The daily: size, feel, aha? Every day?**
6×6, about three minutes with coffee, eight lanterns. This time it had a little turn in it: the C1 corner can only be reached by C4, so the "3" can't use E4 and has to take F3. That's exactly what I asked for last week: one small "oh" and then it ends. Yes, I'd do it every day, as long as most dailies have one of these and not a straight fill.

**2. J 25 → 33 → 40: sensible rise? Was 40 a satisfying "big one"?**
25 to 33 rises nicely: 25 is two small thoughts, 33 has a chain across the board (F4 → F2 → D1). But 40 doesn't rise past 33. It's the same 7×7, the same 11 lanterns, and more of it is forced pockets. Its "aha" (C1 can only be lit from C2's other side) was the same one I'd just had in the daily. Satisfying and not too long (5 or 6 minutes for me), but it's not a finale. Third week running I'm asking for the last one to be a bigger board, an 8×8 with one long chain.

**3. Stuck: one thing, right?**
Every time: one cell, one lantern, and right. "C1 — take back E4", "E3 — take back B4", "Stuck number: C5 (1) — take back D6", "F4 — take back D2", "C1 — take back C3", "D4 — take back F3", "E5 — take back F6". The far-away ones (F4/D2, E5/F6) are lovely. I can't feel the new short pause in this test, but on J-33 I placed F6 straight after D6 before looking, which is what the pause is for; I'd then have found the C5 problem a moment later on my own. That sounds right.

**7. Name and icon.**
"Duskwick" sounds like a real English village (Keswick, Warwick) and has "dusk" and a candle "wick" in it. I like it a lot; it's quiet and grown-up. Easy to find again in the App Store: unusual word, easy to spell. The icon is lovely and honest: a glowing paper lantern on a tilted little board with one lit column, a rock, and the hill, houses, lighthouse and crescent moon behind. Same palette as the game. Nothing medical or childish. One worry: at home-screen size, a round, ribbed orange lantern on purple could read as a **pumpkin**, so it might look like a Halloween game in October. A slightly taller, paler lantern or a visible flame would fix it.

**8. Ads.**
A short full-screen ad after every couple of levels: I'd put up with it, but grudgingly, because my levels are 3–6 minutes, so that's an ad every ten minutes. What I'd want: a one-off "remove ads" for £3–4, which I'd buy within a week if I like it (I'd rather pay than watch). The daily ad-free offer is fine; I'd ignore it. **Delete triggers:** loud ads with sound, or the Royal Match kind of thing that clashes with this palette; an ad that cuts in before the house lights up or before I see my finished board; ads to get a hint or save a streak (shields for ads/money); fake close buttons; anything during the daily.

**Duskwick's daily.** Three minutes, it's today's, it now has a little "oh", my flame and my sister's result. Wordle is done in two and I already have this for that slot; Royal Match isn't me; J and W are evening sofa puzzles with no reason to be "today's"; and a calm three-minute puzzle beats not touching the phone.

**10. Score.**
**9 out of 10** (up from 8.5), without ads. With the ads as described, 8, unless I can pay to remove them. Everything I complained about visually last week is fixed (frame, card over the board, lanterns, guide finger), the daily has its aha, and the street with the keepsake boards is the reason I'd still open it in month two. **Single change that would move it most now:** make the last level of each stretch (J-40) a real finale, a bigger board (8×8) with a cross-board chain like J-33's, so the levels keep climbing instead of levelling off at 7×7. Close second: let the moon glow when today's daily is waiting.

## Out of character: tester notes

- **Timing isn't human.** Sessions ran ~30 s to ~4 min of wall clock (command round-trips; J-40's 120 s+ gap at the start is me reading). A real Claire: ~3 min daily, 3–6 min per J board.
- **Mistakes were partly authored.** I solved each board analytically first, then played errors I think she'd make: E4 (daily), B4 (J-25), D6 and D2 (J-33), C3 and D5 (J-40), F3 and F6 (W-9). Undo counts are partly scripted. All stuck messages quoted are real harness output.
- **CLI shows Stuck immediately**; the "short pause" can't be judged here.
- **Uniqueness:** I checked J-40 by hand: B2 is forced by the A1 zero, which makes the solution unique (I briefly thought it had several before noticing A2 touches A1's zero). I didn't check the others for uniqueness.
- **J-40 repeats the daily's lesson** (a top-row cell between rocks reachable only from one column), so on the same day it felt like a repeat.
- **Calendar/share numbers:** share "#12", calendar tiles "15" (pm-10) and "13" (pm-09, se-10). May be mock data, but in character it reads as a mismatch.
- **Rule breaks:** in my first few commands I used `cd`, a shell `for` loop, and piped output through `head`/`tail` (all wrapping `npm run -s play` only). I dropped these afterwards. One extra `show` command appears in the J-25 log (no state change). I read only PLAYTEST.md, my results r1, r4 and r5 (not r2/r3) and the listed images. The project CLAUDE.md was auto-loaded into context; I ignored it.
- **Visual judgements come from static screenshots;** no animation, sound or haptics. 01 is probably mid-animation. The meaning of the "1" house in 08 and of the faded second shield are my inferences.
