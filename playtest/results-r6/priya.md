## Answers (in character)

**1. The daily.**
Size: 6×6, eight lanterns, about three minutes. That's one cup of tea, perfect. It had a real little "aha". The top square trapped between the "2" and the "0" can only be lit from four rows down. When I saw that, I felt clever, and it wasn't a strain. Yes, I'd do it every day. It's the right weight for a weekday.

**2. J 25 → 33 → 40.**
In cleverness, it rose about right, but not in size: all three were 7×7 again, with ten or eleven lanterns. 40 had the best puzzle (the "2" in the top-left that wants the *other* two sides, so the column gets lit from D3). It felt like a proper think, and it was still under five minutes, which is great. But it didn't *feel* like "the big one". Same board, same size, no fanfare. 25 was actually the one that cost me a hint, so in my head it went 25 (hard bit) → 33 (easy) → 40 (clever). I'd still like a slightly bigger board, or a "special" house, every tenth level.

**3. When stuck, did "Stuck:" point at ONE thing, and was it right?**
It always named one lantern, and six times out of seven it was the right one:
- Daily: "take back E4"
- J-25: "take back B4"
- J-33: "take back C1"
- J-40: "take back C3"
- W-9: "take back F3", then "take back F6"

The one miss was J-25. It told me to take back A5, but my real mistake was C7. When I took A5 away, the line said "none", although the "1" next to it could no longer be done. That's the only time I felt lost today, and the hint fixed it straight away. Waiting a moment before it pops up is nicer. It feels less like it's watching me.

**7. Name and icon.**

- **Icon:** a glowing orange lantern on lit tiles, with a pebble, a crescent moon, a lighthouse and little houses. It fits the game exactly.
- **Anything off-putting?** Not childish or medical. In late October, though, a round orange glowing thing under a crescent moon reads a tiny bit pumpkin and Halloween. A more golden or yellow lantern would avoid that.

**8. Ads.**
The once-a-day "watch one for 30 ad-free minutes" is fine; it's my choice.

A full-screen ad after every couple of levels is a lot. My levels are three minutes, so that's an ad every six minutes on my lunch break. I'd put up with every three or four levels if it's skippable within five seconds and silent. What I'd really do is pay £2.99 once to remove ads. I'd buy that the first week, like a starter pack.

What would make me delete it:
- ads with sound blaring in the staff room;
- 30-second unskippable ones;
- ads after the daily, or popping up mid-board;
- fake "game" ads that trick you into tapping;
- lives or energy timers.

**9. Tomorrow morning, 10 minutes: this game's daily.**
Three minutes with the kettle, a streak, a lit house and a ✨ to send my sister. If there's time before the school run, one J level for my street. Not Royal Match: ten minutes doesn't get you anywhere. Not Wordle: this has replaced it. Never "don't touch the phone".
