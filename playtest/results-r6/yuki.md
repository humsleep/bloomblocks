**1. The daily: size, feel, "aha"? Every day?**
It was 6×6 with 8 lanterns, about 4–5 minutes on a real phone. This time it had a real small "aha". The 3 is already two-thirds full from the 2 next to it. Then the little corner under the 0 can only be lit from one far cell. I made one wrong guess first, and that made the "aha" feel earned. It was better than last week's daily, which was a straight chain. Yes, I would do it every day on the train.

**2. J 25 → 33 → 40: did the difficulty rise? Was 40 a satisfying "big one"?**
It rose a little, by smarter clues, not by size. 25 was gentle and 33 was easy (many 0s do the work for you). 40 was the deepest, with the trapped corner at C1. But all three were 7×7 with 10–11 lanterns. 40 was satisfying and not too long (on a phone, maybe 7–8 minutes). It was **not** a "big one". It felt like the same size as 25. I still miss the 8×8 from two weeks ago, especially for before bed.

**3. When stuck: did "Stuck:" point at ONE thing, and was it right?**
Yes, 5 times out of 5, and right every time: "C1 — take back E4", "E3 — take back B4", "F4 — take back D2", "C1 — take back C3", "E5 — take back F6". Each time, one dark cell and one lantern to lift. It is like a quiet teacher. I can't judge the short pause in text, but the waiting idea is good. I want to see my mistake myself first, then get the help.

**7. The name "Duskwick" and the icon.**
- **The name:** "Dusk" I half know (夕暮れ). "Wick" I don't know; I had to be told it is the string in a candle. I can't say it easily (ダスクウィック?), and I would not remember how to spell it. To find it again in the App Store I would type "lantern puzzle" or "ランタン パズル", or look for the icon. It's not off-putting, just hard for me.
- **The icon:** Beautiful and calm. It fits the game exactly: lantern, board, stone, moon, houses. It doesn't look childish or medical. One small worry: a round, ribbed orange lantern can look a bit like a pumpkin at small size, so in October it might read as a Halloween game. A slightly taller lantern shape (like a chōchin) would avoid that.
- **Does it fit?** Yes. "Dusk" fits the purple evening sky very well.

**8. Ads.**
A short full-screen ad after every couple of levels is acceptable if it is silent (or muted when my phone is on silent), closes after a few seconds, and never comes in the daily. On the train, sound is the worst thing. The once-a-day "watch one for 30 ad-free minutes" offer is fine if it is a small quiet button and not a pop-up. I would rather pay once (around ¥500–800) to remove ads forever, and I would.
**I would delete it for:** loud ads with sound, ads when I open the app, ads with tiny or fake ✕ buttons, 30-second ads I can't skip, flashy game ads (like Royal Match) that break the calm, or any ad in the daily.

**9. Tomorrow morning, 10 minutes: one pick.**
**This game's daily.** It's "today's" puzzle, the same one my daughter does, it's calm, and this one had a small "aha". It takes 5 minutes, and then I can tap the tower for one more. Not Wordle: it's English words. Not Royal Match: too noisy. Not J or W alone: no "today" feeling. Not "don't touch the phone": this is my small quiet luxury.

**10. Score.**
**9 / 10** (same as last week). The stuck help is perfect, the shields are clear now, the guide lanterns glow, the daily had an "aha", and the street with its keepsake boards is a reason to stay for months.
- **The single change that would help most now:** give me a real "big one" now and then, for example an 8×8 every tenth tower level, or on Sunday. Right now everything is 6×6 or 7×7, and after months it could all feel the same size.
- **Second:** hide the time by default, on the result card too.

## Out of character: tester notes

- **Rules I broke:**
  - I piped `npm run -s play` output through `head` and `sed -n` to shorten it.
  - I used `cd … &&`, shell variables and `for` loops to run several moves in one Bash call.
  - Apart from that I ran no other commands, and I read only `tools/PLAYTEST.md`, my round-5 notes and the listed images.
  - The project CLAUDE.md was auto-loaded into my context, and I ignored it.
- **My solving:** I solved by text deduction. Each wrong try (daily E4, J-25 B4, J-33 D2, J-40 C3, W-9 F6) was a plausible human first guess, and I placed it to test the Stuck line. Daily E4 was a genuine oversight on my part: I missed that C1 needs C4. The harness times (18–139 s) don't reflect phone play.
- **Stuck line:** It named exactly one cell and one lantern in all 5 cases, and the lantern was always the right one to remove. In no case did it fall back to the "(light from …; rocks …)" form.
- **Difficulty:** J-25, J-33 and J-40 are all 7×7 with 10–11 lanterns. J-33 is the easiest of the three because of its 0 column. So the "big one" at 40 is not bigger in size, only in depth. The daily and W-9 are 6×6 with 8 lanterns. The daily and J-40 both turn on the same kind of trap: a cell at C1 sealed by a 0 or a full number, which can only be lit from one spot.
- **Screenshot issues:**
  - pm-01 shows no guide overlay at all, just a bare board. It may have been captured before the animation.
  - pm-03 and a11y-03 show dashed rings and sparkles on the 3 and the 2 as well as on the stuck cell. That reads as three highlighted things, not one.
  - pm-12: the guide hand toward the moon is very faint, and the moon gives no hint that it can be tapped.
  - The shield cover reads as a cupcake or jellyfish.
  - The default share setting still includes the timer.
- **Icon:** At small sizes the round, ribbed orange lantern may read as a pumpkin.
