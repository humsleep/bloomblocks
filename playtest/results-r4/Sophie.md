## Out of character: tester notes

- **Mistakes were deliberately steered,** as in earlier rounds: I solved each board mentally, then made plausible first-instinct taps (the outer three sides of a 3, the square next to two numbers, corner squares). The reds, undos and the J-12 hint come from those choices. Counts are indicative, not measured.
- **J-12 hint counter shows 2, but Sophie used one.** My first `hint` call's output was cut off by my own output filtering (the message prints on the last line, after the legend). I re-ran `hint` to read it. Treat it as 1 hint.
- **J-12 had no Stuck line at all.** The trouble showed only as `D4 2⊘` the moment D2's three lanterns went on (D1 C2 E2). E2's column light makes E4 illegal and D2 being full rules out D3. So ⊘ was correct and early, but for a player it's an unexplained symbol on a number she didn't touch. Worth linking ⊘ to the lantern responsible, the same way Stuck now does.
- **The J-16 Stuck reason degraded into a full list:** `Stuck: C2 (light from A1 F3 D4 B6 E6; rocks G1 C3 G3), G2 (...)`. The real culprit was A1 alone (it lit A2, the only legal source for row 2). In J-30 every Stuck line named exactly one lantern, and it was the right one. Suggest ranking or limiting the list to the lantern(s) whose removal re-opens a source.
- **Possible non-unique solutions:** J-12 accepts a lantern on either F2 or F5 for the F column (I used F5). W-4 seems to admit both {A3} and {A1, C3} in the top-left (I used A1+C3). Worth checking with a solver if uniqueness is intended.
- **W-4 is identical to round 3's W-4,** so Sophie's clean run reflects memory.
- **Screenshot inconsistencies:** satisfied 0 gold in 04 vs white in 05–07; result card 🔥5 vs share text 🔥3; overfull marker uses up-chevrons; guide 01–03 lanterns unlit vs 00 lit; share sheet in Korean (simulator locale) and no messaging apps; the daily start screen has no "daily"/date cue.
- **Rule deviations:** besides `npm run -s play`, I ran `cat`/`ls` to read PLAYTEST.md and my own past notes, `ls` on the shots folder (which showed two unlisted files, 05-gray and 11-gentle-7x7, that I did not open), and `mkdir -p` for this results folder. The auto-loaded project CLAUDE.md was ignored for judging. No other files opened.
- **Log times are not human times.** The "since start" values include my reading time, and there were no real interruptions.
