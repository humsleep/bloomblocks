## Answers (in character)

**1. Did the J levels get harder as you went? Where?**
Mostly, yes. 12 was a warm-up with two little fixes. 16 was harder: bigger board, and the top-left corner tricked me. 20 dipped: easier than 16, mostly little cells that can only light themselves, so it felt like tidying. 30 was clearly the hardest and the best: 8×8, a field of zeros, and three corrections that each led to the next. So harder overall, with a soft spot at 20.

**2. Stuck and couldn't tell why? Did the "Stuck:" line point to the right thing?**
Four times out of five, yes, and quickly. J-12 "D5 (light from F2)", J-30 "C4 (light from D7)", "C3 (light from E2)" and "G3 (light from H2)" all named my exact lantern first. That's exactly what was missing last week. The one miss was J-16: "C2, G2 (light from A1 F3 D4 B6 E6; rocks G1 C3 G3)". When it names five lanterns and three rocks, it's naming the whole board, so I couldn't tell which one mattered. It was A1. If several apply, put the most guilty one first, or only show that one.

**3. Hints and undos: what happened right after?**
Hint on J-16: it pointed straight at A1. Within a few seconds I saw why (row 2 had only one legal spot, A2), and I finished in three taps. Again it told me what, and the why came by itself, which I like. Undos (E2 on J-12, E5 on J-16, F1 on J-20, H2 on J-30) were all "oops, that clashes" or "that blocks a pocket"; I fixed them at once and carried on.

## Out of character: tester notes

- **Timing isn't human.** Session times (J-12 ~6 min, J-16 ~5 min, J-20 ~4.5 min, J-30 and W-4 under 1 min) reflect command round-trips and my reading pace, not her thinking. A real Claire would take several minutes on J-30.
- **Mistakes were partly authored.** I solved each board analytically, then played errors I think she'd make (D5 central and E2/F2 on J-12; E5 and A1 corner on J-16; F1 on J-20; D7, E2, H2 on J-30). Undo/hint counts are therefore partly scripted. The stuck messages after each one are real harness output.
- **J-16 hint was a judgement call.** I knew A2 was needed; she plausibly wouldn't, given the stuck line named 5 lanterns + 3 rocks. I didn't capture the hint's full wording (my output filter cut it); the session log records cell A1.
- **Stuck-reason length:** `Stuck: C2 (light from A1 F3 D4 B6 E6; rocks G1 C3 G3), G2 (same)` on J-16. Only A1 was the culprit; the others are technically "involved". Ranking or trimming would help.
- **J-12 stuck after D5:** `C6 (light from B4 A5; rock C5)`. The real cause was my D5 filling the "1" at C5; the rock is named, so it worked, but my lantern D5 isn't named directly.
- **W-4 is the identical board to round 3**, so it measured memory, not difficulty. A real returning player would also remember it.
- **Streak mismatch:** screenshot 09 shows streak 5, the given share text shows 🔥3. May just be different mock data.
- **Stray `show` commands** appear in the J-16 and J-20 logs (I re-displayed the board); they don't change state.
- **Visual judgements** come from static screenshots only; no animation, sound or haptics. The meaning of the dashed rings, the double chevron in 05, and the two small lanterns in 09 are my inferences.
