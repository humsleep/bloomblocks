## Out of character: tester notes

- **ASCII vs phone:** I played from text with cell names and an explicit `Stuck:` line plus ✓/↑/✗/⊘ marks. The phone shows only the thin string and colors (04). On the phone, real Denise would take longer, especially on J-30 (8×8: smaller cells, a pale string).
- **Mistakes were partly chosen.** I could solve every board in my head (all have short forced chains). I played her habits: biggest number first, "obvious" side, ignore zeros, corner first, tap dark pockets. The errors (D5/E4/E2 on J-12; E5, G4, G6, A1 on J-16; C2, F1 on J-20; F6, D7, C4, E2, A1 on J-30; C5, D2, A5 on W-4) were persona-plausible, not genuine confusion.

The real culprit was A1 (A2 was needed to light G2), but the list didn't single it out. Ranking or highlighting the one most to blame would help.

J-20 (7×7) ended in five isolated single-cell pockets (A3, F3, E5, D7, F7), each a forced tap, so it's trivial once the numbers are done. J-30 (8×8, 46 floor cells, 13 lanterns) is mostly longer, not deeper.
