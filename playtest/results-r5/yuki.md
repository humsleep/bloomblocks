**1. The daily: how long did it feel, was it the right size, would I do it every day?**
On a real phone it would take me about 4–5 minutes. Without mistakes there was nothing to think about twice. That is the right size for the start of my train ride, and it leaves time for a tower puzzle. It's a little too easy for me alone, but my daughter could do it too, and that is part of the point. I would do it every day, but I would want a slightly harder one on some days (a 7×7 at the weekend, maybe).

**2. Did the J levels get harder in a sensible way? Was level 30 the "big one"?**
22, 30 and 35 were all 7×7 with 10–11 lanterns, so they felt about the same size. They got harder through smarter numbers, not through size. 35 was the deepest, with many numbers working together. Level 30 was **not** a "big one" this time. Last week level 30 was 8×8 with 13 lanterns and felt like a peak. This week it was a normal 7×7, no bigger than 22. It was satisfying, but not special. It was also not too long. For me, an 8×8 before bed is fine, and on the train 7×7 is better.

**3. When stuck: did the "Stuck:" line point at ONE thing? Was it right?**
Yes, every time, and every time it was right. "take back D4", "take back D3", "take back D6" and "Stuck number: C4 (2) — take back D5". The long lists from last week (five lanterns and three rocks) are gone. Now it reads like a teacher tapping one spot on the board. In J-30 it named three dark cells but one lantern, which is fine because one lantern caused all three.
