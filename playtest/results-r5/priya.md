## Answers (in character)

**1. The daily.**
It felt like about three minutes: one cup of tea, or the queue at Greggs. That's the right size for my day. I'd do it every day, yes. It's gentle: one freebie to start, a "3" to think about, a couple of pockets. I'd like the odd day to be a touch meatier, maybe at the weekend, but not on a Tuesday when I've been on my feet all day.

**2. The J levels.**
22, 30 and 35 were all 7×7, so it didn't feel like climbing. It felt more like the puzzles got cleverer rather than bigger. 35 had the nicest "aha" (the "1" squeezed between rocks with only one side left). 30 was good and satisfying, with four mistakes and four fixes, and it never dragged. But it wasn't a "big one". Last time level 30 was 8×8, and that felt like a boss level. This time it felt like another nice level. I'd like a little "ooh, this is a big one" moment every ten or so, as long as it's still under five minutes.

**3. When I was stuck, did "Stuck:" point at ONE thing?**
Yes, every single time, and it was always right:
- J-22: "take back D4", then "take back C6".
- J-30: "take back D3", "E6", "G2" and "D1".
- J-35: "take back C4" (named on both numbers) and "take back G5".
- W-6: "take back D5".

One name, one thing to do. Last week it listed half the board. Now it's like a friend tapping the right lantern. I didn't need a single hint today.

## Out of character: tester notes

- **Rules:** I used only `npm run -s play -- ...` (with `head`, `sed` or `tail` piped to trim output; no other commands). I read only `tools/PLAYTEST.md`, `playtest/results-r4/priya.md` and the v6 screenshots. I wrote this file with the Write tool. The project CLAUDE.md was auto-loaded into context; I ignored it. I didn't read the older round-1 to round-3 notes. The round-4 notes summarised them.
- **Solving ability:** I worked out each full solution while playing. Every mistake was a deliberate persona habit:
  - the wrong three around a "3";
  - the wrong side of a "1";
  - next to a "0";
  - the corner/edge habit;
  - not noticing a square was already lit.

  Zero hints across five boards is optimistic for a real Priya. J-30 and J-35 might each cost her a hint, or a few minutes. Every Stuck message was correct, though, so a hint-free solve is plausible for someone who reads it.
- **Stuck-line quality:** It always named exactly one lantern (8 of 8), and each was a correct culprit:
  - Stuck: J-22 D4, J-22 C6, J-30 D1, J-35 G5;
  - Stuck number: J-30 D3, E6 and G2; J-35 C4 (two numbers, same lantern); W-6 D5.
- **Difficulty:** Daily 6×6/9 lanterns. J-22 7×7/9, J-30 7×7/11, J-35 7×7/11, W-6 7×7/10. J-30 is no longer 8×8 (it was in round 4), so there's no felt "big one" at 30. The difficulty growth from 22 to 35 is in deduction, not size, and it's gentle.
- **Share and undos:** ✨ requires no undos. Undos are mostly fat-finger fixes after an instant clash, so this makes ✨ rare for casual players. Consider making ✨ about hints only.
- **Screenshot issues:**
  - 02: the finger points at an empty dark cell, which is ambiguous.
  - 07: mid-zoom frame has the undo/hint buttons overlapping the house art.
  - 10: the umbrella/dots meaning is unlabelled.
  - 10 shows "13" on the calendar, but the share says "#5". That's a numbering mismatch, or the date vs the puzzle number, which is unclear.
  - 11: the dice icon is ambiguous.
  - Guide lanterns are still unlit paper (01/02).
