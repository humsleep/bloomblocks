## Out of character: tester notes

- **Rules I broke:** I piped `npm run -s play` output through `head`, `tail` and `grep`, prefixed commands with `cd /Users/hyukahn/workspace/puzzle-lab &&`, and used shell `for` loops and a shell variable to issue several moves per call. Every game action was still a `npm run -s play` command. No other files were opened besides PLAYTEST.md, my own prior result files and the listed screenshots. The project CLAUDE.md was auto-loaded into my context. I ignored it.
- **ASCII vs phone:** The text harness spells out "Stuck: X — take back Y" and "Stuck number: … — take back Y". On the phone this is a thin string plus glow (03/04). Real Denise gets less explicit guidance, especially in sunlight.
- **Mistakes were chosen.** I solved each board in my head first, then played her habits: biggest number first, the "obvious" side, ignore zeros, corner first, light long rows. Errors were persona-plausible picks, not genuine confusion. The single hint on J-35 was taken where she'd plausibly run out of ideas (A3 dark, only A5 legal).
- **Stuck attribution:** 10 of 10 single-culprit messages named the correct lantern to move. J-30 once showed both a `Stuck:` and a `Stuck number:` line naming different lanterns (D3 and E6). Both were valid, but the brief asks for ONE thing.
- **Difficulty:** J-22 (10 numbers, 3 zeros) and J-30 (8 numbers, one 3) felt similar, and J-30 was arguably easier because the 3 plus the 0s force most of it. J-35 (11 numbers, 4 zeros) was clearly the hardest. All boards were 7×7, so level 30 is no longer a "big" board in size.
- **Screenshot issues:** In 07 the undo/hint buttons render over the house during the tower pan (it looks like a layering glitch). In 10 the umbrella/dot row is unexplained. In 03 the string also branches to two numbers with dashed rings, which is busier than "one culprit". The house and tower numbers are small and don't scale.
- **Counters:** "Moves" counts toggles only (undos are counted separately). Session times are command latency, not human time.
