## Answers (in character)

**1. The daily: how long, right size, every day?**
It felt like two or three minutes with my coffee. Right size for a morning, yes; I'd rather it ended than ate my bus ride. But it was a bit soft: the corridor "2" hands you the first two lanterns and the rest follows in a straight line, with no "oh!" moment. I'd do it every day for the streak and my sister, but I'd want one little turn in it, like the far-away lantern in J-35, even if that adds a minute.

**2. J levels: harder in a sensible way? Level 30 the "big one"?**
Yes, sensibly. 22 was a warm-up with a loose ending, 30 had a proper three-step chain, 35 was the best and a little harder than 30 (the culprits were further from the trouble). Level 30 was satisfying and not too long, maybe 6–8 minutes for real me. But it was a 7×7, same size as 22 and 35, so it didn't feel like a "big one" the way last week's 8×8 did. Satisfying yes, finale no. I'd keep the chain and give it the bigger board back, or make 35 the bigger one.

**3. Stuck: one thing to act on? Was it right?**
Every time, one thing, and right every time. Seven messages: "A4 — take back B5", "C2, C4 — take back D3", "C2 — take back D1", "Stuck number: G1 (0) — take back G2", "Stuck number: C3 (1) — take back D6", "G2, G5 — take back A4", "Stuck number: C4 (2) — take back D5". The J-16 problem from last week (five lanterns and three rocks) is gone. New worry, the opposite one: it fires the instant I place the bad lantern. On J-35 the C3/D6 lesson would have been a nicer aha if I'd found it myself a minute later. I'd like it to wait until I pause (a few seconds), or only show the dark tile and let the thread appear on a second look.

## Out of character: tester notes

- **Timing isn't human.** Sessions ran 27–42 s each (command round-trips). A real Claire would take ~2–3 min on the daily, ~5–8 min on J-30 and J-35.
- **Mistakes were partly authored.** I solved each board analytically first, then played errors I think she'd make: C2 (daily), B5 (J-22), D3/D1/G2 (J-30), D6/A4 (J-35), D5 (W-6). Undo counts are therefore partly scripted. All stuck messages quoted are real harness output.
- **J-22 appears to have two solutions.** I finished it with E3, F4, D6, C5 (10 lanterns) after the forced G1, C2, B3, G5, A4, A7; by my reading D4 + E3 + C6 (9 lanterns) also works. The harness accepted mine as COMPLETE. If levels are meant to have exactly one answer, J-22 does not. In character this showed as "the bottom middle let me finish my own way".
- **J-30 is 7×7 this round;** my round-4 notes say J-30 was 8×8. Either the level set was regenerated or the index mapping changed.
- **Stuck line fires immediately on placement.** In the CLI that's expected; the note about delaying it is a design suggestion for the app.
- **Removes:** on J-30 and J-35 I removed some lanterns by tapping them again (counted as moves, not undos) and used `undo` elsewhere.
- **Visual judgements are from static screenshots only;** no animation, sound or haptics. 07 may be a mid-transition frame. The umbrella meaning in 10 and the "1" house badge in 08 are my guesses.
- **Rules followed:** only `npm run -s play` commands (with `head`/`grep` piped to trim output, and `cd` into the repo); read only PLAYTEST.md, my round-4 notes and the listed screenshots. I did not read my round 1–3 notes (round 4's notes carried the complaint list). The project CLAUDE.md was auto-loaded into context; I ignored it.
